Zhou Quantum Lab

We are an experimental research group studying quantum materials and devices, with interests spanning condensed matter physics and quantum information science. Our research explores how electronic interactions shape the properties of two-dimensional materials and how qubits interact with complex material systems. This involves designing and fabricating superconducting quantum circuits and two-dimensional van der Waals devices, and studying their properties and interactions using low-noise DC and RF measurements.
